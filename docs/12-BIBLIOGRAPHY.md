## Systematic review protocol
Search ACL Anthology, Google Scholar, Crossref, relevant Islamic-studies journals, and formal methods libraries for Quran ontology/semantic graph, Quranic Arabic annotation, formal Islamic legal reasoning, textual variants, proof assistants, deontic logic and hadith contradiction. Record search strings, dates, inclusion criteria, evidence of peer review, data and code availability, and limitations. Compare existing projects against provenance, ambiguity handling, proof checking, semantics, coverage and circularity safeguards. Distinguish preprints from peer-reviewed work. Do not claim novelty before comparison.
