# 00 — Charter
Status: draft for review, 2026-11-27.

## Aim
Develop a reproducible, versioned framework that traces every formal claim to a precise Quranic source and distinguishes valid deductive consequences from unresolved interpretation. Explore whether specific propositions extracted from hadith contradict reviewed Quranic interpretations, without equating consistency with authenticity.

## Research questions
1. Which Quranic speech acts can be faithfully represented in specified logics?
2. How can competing readings, syntactic parses, referents and contextual scopes be represented without suppressing ambiguity?
3. How can an independently reproducible proof or countermodel be attached to a comparison?
4. Which conclusions remain stable across explicitly admitted interpretation profiles?
5. How do new interpretations or source revisions invalidate earlier results?

## Scope
Initial corpus: standard Hafs 'an Asim edition with Kufan numbering (114 surahs, 6236 numbered verses), with a schema designed for other canonical transmissions and numbering systems. These numbers describe the initial edition, not all traditions. All verses are to be indexed; semantic coverage is incremental, not presumed. Quran-first is an explicit project methodology, not a claim to settle theological debates about the Sunnah.

## Non-goals
- No automated religious rulings, judgments about persons, claims of perfect interpretation, or declaration of fabricated hadith solely from logical results.
- No replacing Arabic with a translation; no treating each verse as one axiom.
- No use of an LLM as proof checker or final linguistic authority.
- No assuming absence of proof implies falsity or consistency.
- No silently mixing readings, theological commitments, or interpretive methods.

## Deliverables
Versioned corpus manifests and validation reports, interpretation records, typed semantic intermediate representation, proof-producing verification interface, small reviewed pilot, benchmark and limitations report. Long-term full coverage means documented speech-act and interpretation coverage, not a proof of complete divine meaning.

## Success measures
Traceability, reproducibility, explicitly bounded claims, human review, sound inference relative to declared semantics, meaningful negative controls, and published unresolved cases. Coverage and review quality are reported separately. No target percentage of certainty is invented.
