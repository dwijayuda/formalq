# 07 — Hadith comparison specification
Status: proposed; no religious or authenticity judgments.

## Separate assessments
A hadith record has collection/edition/book/number, Arabic matn, isnad, variant family, narrator and grading references (source-specific), exact citations and provenance. Historical authenticity assessment is independent from formal compatibility. A sahih grading does not automatically resolve interpretation; compatibility does not authenticate a narration.

Record a source's grading as an attributed scholarly assessment with exact edition, locator, terminology, method when available, and the variant, chain, or textual component to which it applies. Keep variant-family links as sourced and possibly contested. Preserve exact quoted spans inside the matn and chain, including reported speech within a report. Apparent agreement with the Quran can coexist with divergent historical assessments. Historical criticism can include scrutiny of content as well as transmission. FormalQ has no rule mapping a comparison outcome to a historical grade. [Brown 2008](https://www.drjonathanbrown.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/08/How-We-Know-Early-Hadith-Critics-Did-Matn-Criticism-and-Why-Its-So-Hard-to-Find.pdf)

Extract candidate propositions with speaker, audience, normative/descriptive status, time, conditions and alternative readings. A report may contain multiple claims. Never infer that the Quran endorses a claim merely because it is not contradicted. Compare matched contexts; report missing context as unresolved. Record any shared evidence dependencies to avoid circularity.

Compare a specific matn claim and interpretation revision. A reviewed context bridge states the corresponding sense, time, domain and speech-act force, and identifies any substantive premises needed by the bridge. A possible dependence on the target report, its family or an inherited interpretation triggers the review in the [methodology](01-METHODOLOGY.md). Restrict Quran-relative comparison when material ancestry remains unresolved. A report-to-report relation or an embedding match can help locate variants for review.

## Per-profile outcome policy
Apply the shared [evidence and precedence specification](15-VERIFICATION-SPEC.md). Keep proof and model evidence separate. Reserve entailment and refutation labels for a checked proof direction with a satisfiability witness for the exact theory. When only a derivation is available, show it as conditional on the unresolved base theory. Record joint satisfiability for an explicit matching model, and independence only with models for both polarities. Block classification on inconsistent theory or evidence-integrity failure. Record coverage, context and consistency evidence on every result.

## Across profiles
Return each reviewed profile and claim reading as a separate result. Aggregate over the explicitly named comparable pairs and list unassessed alternatives. If two results disagree, identify whether profile premises, claim reading or context alignment changed. A formal conflict addresses the specified claim under the stated premises. Historical assessments remain independently attributed.

## Report template
Include the hadith artifact, claim and revision; Arabic span; Quran edition and source spans; argument and context or bridge; evidence dependencies; theory and signature hashes; calculus and toolchain; proof/model artifacts and checked assumptions; consistency and coverage; review records and limitations. Where historical assessments are included, cite the source and its scope in a separate field and keep the comparative status attached to the formal claim.

## Evaluation
Use the synthetic logic controls and also test indirect circularity via a hypothetical commentary → variant family dependency, report speaker versus quoted speaker, and two otherwise identical reports with different time scopes. Include both reviewed independence from a report and unresolved possible dependence. Real examples require qualified review and documented case selection.
