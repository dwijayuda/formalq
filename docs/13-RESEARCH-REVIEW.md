# 13 — G0 research review

Status: targeted review for design discussion, 2026-11-27. The baseline was the 14 Markdown files at commit `a830ab78be717190e2e704e8d8f806bbe0e87d5a`. This review proposes research priorities and falsifiable design requirements. Domain reviewers and the maintainer still need to assess them.

## Review method and limits

Publisher records, author copies, source documentation and formal-library repositories were consulted. The search covered Arabic annotation and Quran/Hadith ontologies, qirāʾāt and verse counting, legal hermeneutics and discourse, hadith criticism, and formalized first-order logic. Representative reproducible queries were `"Dukes" "Morphological Annotation of Quranic Arabic"`, `"Towards a Joint Ontology of Quran and Hadith"`, `"Corpus Coranicum" variant readings 1 4`, `"How We Know Early Hadith Critics Did Matn Criticism"`, and `Lean 4 first order logic Foundation soundness completeness`. [Bibliography and source details](12-BIBLIOGRAPHY.md)

Selection favored primary work with clear implications for the design. Access ranged from full documentation and code interfaces to publisher abstracts and bibliographic records. The review did not exhaust classical commentary or establish comparative annotation accuracy, publisher permissions, or novelty. The next literature pass should record result screening and compare exact editions and schools for the pilot passages before proposing a substantive premise.

## Findings that change the design

| Weakness in the baseline | Finding and consequence |
|---|---|
| Evidence classes alone could permit downstream import of external assumptions. | The Quranic Arabic Corpus ontology explicitly draws on hadith and Ibn Kathīr. It links some pronouns to concepts. Treat those imported links as interpretive proposals with provenance. A graph edge cannot silently enter a Quran-relative theory. The joint ontology of Altammami et al. is a close precedent for cross-corpus concept matching. Use its task as a retrieval baseline and evaluate evidence for formal consequences separately. [QAC ontology](https://corpus.quran.com/ontology.jsp), [Altammami et al. 2021](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/171763/) |
| Agreement between Arabic annotations could be mistaken for independent corroboration. | QAC uses segmentation, a traditional-grammar tagset and supervised annotation. MASAQ offers another traditional iʿrāb analysis on Tanzil text. Preserve original segmentation and tagsets, distinguish shared text ancestry from annotation methods, and compare disagreements only after alignment. Annotator counts and column totals cannot establish interpretive fidelity. [Dukes and Habash 2010](https://aclanthology.org/L10-1190/), [Dukes et al. 2013](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/81650/), [MASAQ v6](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/9yvrzxktmr/6), [Sawalha et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2024.111211) |
| A per-verse representation can lose speaker, antecedent, scope and apparent-conflict resolution. | Kamp's discourse representation supplies a candidate method for testing antecedent accessibility across scope boundaries. Al-Shāfiʿī's *Risālah* is a primary comparative source for textual interpretation and reconciliation. Extract precisely cited methods from multiple traditions before authorizing bridge rules. Neither source supplies a default parse or a universal formal rule for FormalQ. [Kamp](https://doi.org/10.1002/9780470758335.ch8), [Al-Shāfiʿī, translated by Lowry](https://nyupress.org/9781479809028/the-epistle-on-legal-theory/) |
| A single “reading” field cannot distinguish textual artifacts, testimony and recitation practices. | Corpus Coranicum distinguishes reports from philological and exegetical sources, including material from al-Dānī, and documents variants at the project’s 1:4 pilot anchor. Van Putten’s historical linguistic study considers the consonantal text and reading traditions. Preserve scholarly attribution, transmission and edition identities on separate axes. Quran.ws alignment can seed a comparison but its shared word ID is an external mapping proposal. [Corpus Coranicum](https://corpuscoranicum.de/en/variants), [1:4 variant entries](https://corpuscoranicum.de/en/verse-navigator/sura/1/verse/4/variants), [Van Putten 2022](https://www.jstor.org/stable/10.1163/j.ctv2gjwx0j), [Quran.ws source repository](https://github.com/quran-ws/quran-text) |
| “Minimal dependencies” could lead to duplicating established metatheory. | Mathlib supplies first-order syntax and realization. Foundation supplies formalized calculi with soundness and completeness. An Isabelle development provides a reference for sound sort erasure. Compare reuse, a proved bridge and a small standalone fragment against the same tests before selecting a Lean design. [Mathlib syntax](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/master/Mathlib/ModelTheory/Syntax.lean), [Foundation](https://github.com/FormalizedFormalLogic/Foundation), [Sound and Complete Sort Encodings](https://isa-afp.org/entries/Sort_Encodings.html) |
| Treating commands as assertions or attaching a one-place necessity operator can change the inferential question. | Chisholm’s contrary-to-duty example gives a regression case for violation and reparation. Compare annotation-only, conditional obligation and defeasible rule approaches. Defer any choice of deontic semantics until a reviewed case and competing consequence patterns exist. [Chisholm 1963](https://doi.org/10.1093/analys/24.2.33) |
| A compatibility label could be read as a historical assessment, while an isnād-only description would also misstate the field. | Brown documents instances of early matn criticism and discusses why some such criticism is hard to identify. FormalQ addresses a narrower question about a selected claim and a profile. Record historical judgments with their sources and variant scope independently, including disagreements. [Brown 2008, author copy](https://www.drjonathanbrown.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/08/How-We-Know-Early-Hadith-Critics-Did-Matn-Criticism-and-Why-Its-So-Hard-to-Find.pdf) |

## Decisions requiring review

Highest priority is reproducible source intake, followed by the admission and circularity rules and then a synthetic proof-and-model qualification. An ontology can support discovery. A proposition with a reviewed path from source to formal assumptions can support deduction. Publish disagreements and failures in addition to successful analyses. Compare the project with existing work on source lineage, alternate parses, bridge semantics, explicit consistency evidence and proof replay. A demonstrated difference on one of these dimensions is evidence about a task, with any novelty claim subject to a broader review.
