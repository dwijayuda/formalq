## Observed structure and disagreements

- **Numbering.** Both Tanzil texts, the KFGQPC v2.0 CSV and JSON, and both annotation datasets cover the same 114 sūrahs and 6,236 verse keys. Tanzil tests account for 1:1, the absence of the opening formula at the beginning of sūrah 9, the first-row prefixes in other sūrahs, the distinct forms at 95:1 and 97:1, and the in-verse formula at 27:30. KFGQPC CSV and JSON agree across all 11 fields after rendering integer cells as decimal strings. KFGQPC's font-dependent terminal marker uses `U+FC00 + (āyah number − 1)` in every row of this export. The separator is NBSP except at 2:286, where it is an ordinary space. [Tanzil's basmala documentation](https://tanzil.net/docs/a_note_on_bismillah)
- **Text comparison.** Exact and NFC equality were 0 of 6,236 for the unadjusted Uthmani pair and 1 of 6,236 for Tanzil Simple versus the KFGQPC search field. A display-boundary diagnostic excludes Tanzil's unnumbered first-row prefixes and the KFGQPC terminal verse markers in memory for comparison. It finds 64 matches in the Uthmani pair and 20 in the Simple/search pair, with the same counts after NFC. Internal marks and spellings remain intact, including any difference due to form options. These are encoding and formatting comparisons. The remaining differences need field-by-field linguistic and editorial analysis before drawing conclusions about reading equivalence or text integrity.
- **QAC.** The mirror has 128,219 segment rows and 77,429 distinct verse-local word positions. It contains 208 empty surface forms carrying the first-person singular suffix annotation. They remain present as records. QAC's online example at 43:68 illustrates why an elided pronoun need not have an overt source span. [QAC analysis of 43:68:1](https://corpus.quran.com/wordmorphology.jsp?location=%2843%3A68%3A1%29)
- **MASAQ.** CSV, TSV, JSON and the main SQLite table agree row for row on 157,676 rows and 19 fields when typed integers are rendered as decimal strings and null cells are compared as empty. SQLite integrity checks passed. The release page describes 20 fields, and the paper's table gives 131,930 morphemes, 123,565 syntactic instances and 77,408 words. The delivered files have 157,254 distinct position tuples, including 422 tuples repeated in the row stream, 110,886 nonempty syntactic-role cells, and 77,411 distinct verse-local word positions. The role lookup contains 73 distinct tag values. These measured units are not assumed equivalent to the paper's units. Preserve all rows and seek an export-to-paper explanation before defining a uniqueness constraint or agreement benchmark. [Release metadata](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/9yvrzxktmr/6), [article and table](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39830618/)
- **Annotation cross-check.** Nineteen verses have different distinct word-position counts in QAC and MASAQ. Equal position tuples are only candidates for comparison. Text-level alignment, segmentation and the shared Tanzil ancestry must be reviewed before scoring agreement or claiming corroboration.
